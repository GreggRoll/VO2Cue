HEART SYNC PREVIEW — version 1.2, build 10

Install build 10 on BOTH iPhone and Apple Watch.

NEW IN BUILD 10
• Onboarding introduces Apple Watch heart-rate tracking with hard and recovery range sliders, explains the workout graph and spoken heart-rate cues, and leads into maximum-HR setup.
• The Watch timeline uses a compact navigation title so the graph and dashed-target caption fit together.
• New workouts use spoken cues by default. Existing saved cue choices remain unchanged; enable Spoken cues in an existing profile if you want to hear guidance.

WHAT TO TEST
1. Open Settings → Review introduction, or install fresh. Check the example sliders, Watch requirement, graph explanation and spoken-cue guidance. Enter a known maximum or use the age estimate, 220 − (0.7 × age), and verify your hard and recovery targets.
2. Start a Watch workout. Compare the current rounded BPM, range marker and target status on both devices. Targets should change between hard and recovery intervals. Check the bottom target text and tap the heart-rate readout to view the timeline; the full caption should fit on the larger Watch. Tap Timer to return.
3. At halfway in either phase, below target must say Heart rate up, within target must say Heart rate good, and above target must say Heart rate down. Enable halfway, heart-rate and spoken cues in the profile. Missing or paused readings must not announce good.
4. Phone-only workouts and records without Watch heart-rate data must have no timeline. A Watch workout's graph appears after its first recorded reading. Check the BPM line and dashed target guides through hard and recovery cycles.
5. Pause/resume, skip, disconnect/reconnect and create a sensor gap. Collected graph history must remain available. Confirm Keep recovery easy, Build gradually toward your target, and the ten-second trend explanation never appear.

Graphs remain local to the paired devices and bounded to 480 points. No simulated heart-rate or screenshot fixture is included in TestFlight. Live Activities show the interval timer; open the app for heart rate and the graph.
