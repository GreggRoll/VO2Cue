HEART SYNC PREVIEW — version 1.2, build 9

Install build 9 on BOTH iPhone and Apple Watch.

NEW IN BUILD 9
• Spoken cues are enabled by default for newly created workouts, templates, the initial Norwegian 4×4 profile and Heart Sync Check on iPhone and Apple Watch.
• Existing saved workout settings are preserved, including Spoken cues switched off. To hear cues in an existing workout, edit its profile and enable Spoken cues.

INCLUDED FROM BUILD 8
• Recovery below the lower target now says Heart rate up. Heart rate good is reserved for readings within the target range, including its boundaries, in both hard and recovery intervals.
• Range status, meter and halfway cues now agree with the current rounded BPM displayed on screen, instead of an older trend. Stale and paused samples still suppress heart-rate cues.
• Removed Keep recovery easy, Build gradually toward your target, and the ten-second trend explanation. Ongoing deviation cues also use Heart rate up/down.
• Workout timelines appear only after Watch heart-rate readings have actually been recorded. Phone-only timers and records without heart-rate data have no timeline. Previously recorded Watch graphs remain visible during pauses and temporary sensor gaps.
• The Watch phase name uses the compact top title area, freeing space for the bottom target text. Tap the heart-rate readout to see its graph once readings have been collected; tap Timer to return.

WHAT TO TEST
1. During recovery, compare below/in/above target status and meter on both devices. At halfway, below must say up, within must say good, and above must say down. Enable halfway, heart-rate and spoken cues in the profile.
2. Confirm the removed coaching/trend messages never appear.
3. Start a phone-only workout: no Workout timeline should appear while running, on completion or in its saved detail. Start a Watch workout without a reading: the graph should appear only after the first recorded reading.
4. Check Sprint/Recover, countdown, BPM, status, meter and bottom target on your Watch screen. Pause/resume, skip, disconnect/reconnect and create a sensor gap. Missing readings must not announce good; collected graph history must remain available.

Maximum-HR setup and the age estimate 220 − (0.7 × age) are unchanged. Watch graphs remain local to the paired devices and bounded to 480 points. No simulated HR source is included in TestFlight. Live Activities show the interval timer; open the app for HR and the graph.
